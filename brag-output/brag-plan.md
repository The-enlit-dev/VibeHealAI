# /brag plan: vibeheal.online

**Input:** the website https://vibeheal.online (rendered headlessly; copy, colours and logo taken from the live page and its own source in `website/`).
**Format:** landscape 1920×1080, 30 fps, 21 s.
**Tone:** `default` (punchy, playful, clean), with the humour coming from the site's own honesty.

## Rubric
- **What is it?** A planned AI assistant for vibe coders: people who build apps, websites and software with AI tools.
- **Who is it for, what does it do for them?** Vibe coders, especially beginners using Claude Code, Cursor and similar tools. It aims to help them understand their projects, connect their tools, automate chores and recover safely when something breaks.
- **What sets it apart?** Honest status labels on everything ("in development", "planned", "exploring"). You approve anything sensitive. It sits alongside your AI coding tool and doesn't replace it.
- **Most impressive / funniest claim:** "Nothing on this site is a launch promise." A launch video for a product whose own site says it hasn't launched.
- **Visual hook:** a terminal filling up with red errors, under the site's own problem lines: "Code you didn't write." / "Errors without a map."
- **Real UI to show:** the hero (logo, headline, "Just vibeheal it."), the four "How it's intended to work" steps, the "Status at a glance" card with its real badges, the "Join the waitlist" button.
- **Tone:** default, warm and confident, with no overclaiming.
- **Share caption:** see `share-copy.txt`.

## Angle
**The honest launch video.** It opens on the pain (a broken project), reveals VibeHeal, shows how it's meant to work, then lands the joke straight from the site: the status card stamps "PLANNED" on almost everything, followed by "Nothing on this site is a launch promise." It ends on the waitlist. Every claim is the site's own copy. Nothing implies the product is available.

**Privacy:** the terminal errors are generic illustrative text (no real paths, names or hosts). The video contains no emails, tokens or personal data.

## Visual identity
- Colours from `brand.css`: navy `#14213D`, teal `#1B998B`, teal-ink `#0E6F64`, ivory `#F6F4EF`, slate `#667085`, amber `#F4B942`.
- Type: the site's system sans stack (it renders as Inter here) with heavy, tight headlines. The site's monospace face is used for eyebrows and badges.
- The real logo SVG: navy tile, three teal squares and an amber dot. It assembles square by square on the beat.

## Music
120 BPM (beat 0.5 s, bar 2 s), D major. Built from scratch in Python as a single stem.
- 0–6 s: tense, filtered, minor colour (B minor). Kick and low pulse only. Error blips are pitched to the key.
- 6 s: the drop at the reveal. Full groove: kick, claps on 2 and 4, offbeat hats, bass, plucked arpeggio and pad, chords D – Bm – G – A.
- 18 s: outro. Groove resolves to D; the final chord rings out to 21 s.
- SFX in key, mixed under the music: soft filtered whooshes on cuts, tuned plucks on logo squares and badge stamps, a soft click on the button.
