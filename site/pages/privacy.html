What the early-access waitlist collects, why, and where it is kept.

What the waitlist collects

  • Your email address (required).
  • Optional answers: your name, the AI coding tool you use, your biggest vibe-coding frustration, and which planned features interest you.
  • Your consent to be emailed about early access, and the date and time you signed up.

Why

Your email is used only to contact you about early access to this project. The optional answers help decide what to build first. Answers are summarised as counts, for example how many people chose each planned feature.

We won't add you to unrelated marketing, and we don't sell your email.

Where it is kept and who can see it

Signups are stored in a private file on the server that runs the waitlist. Only authorised project admins can view them, through a password-protected admin area. Signups are never published on this website or exposed through a public address.

To limit abuse, the server briefly keeps a count of recent attempts per network address in memory. That count is not saved with your signup.

This website does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

How long it is kept

A retention period has not been decided yet. It will be stated here before the waitlist opens to the public.
